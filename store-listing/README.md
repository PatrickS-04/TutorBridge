## Full description

```text
TutorBridge connects students with the tutors available at their institution. Find the right tutor, agree on a time, meet on or off campus, and keep a record of every session, all in one app.

FOR STUDENTS
• See tutors from your own institution, ranked by how well they match your requests
• Post a tuition request and choose from the tutors who offer to help
• Book a time inside a tutor's weekly hours, or set up a recurring booking of up to 24 sessions
• Reschedule or cancel an upcoming session
• Export your completed sessions as a PDF from your learning history

FOR TUTORS
• Publish your weekly availability and confirm or decline bookings
• Apply to tuition requests with your own rate
• Keep a private checklist and notes for each session
• See your hours and earnings for each monthly claim period, and export them as a PDF voucher or CSV file

VERIFIED ATTENDANCE
Tutors show a short-lived code, as a QR code and as text. Students scan or type it on their own phone, and claim vouchers show which sessions were verified this way.

STAY IN TOUCH
• Chat with your tutor or student as soon as a session is arranged
• Get push notifications for new messages and booking changes, and tap one to open the right screen
• Link Google Calendar so confirmed sessions appear on your calendar
• Report or block someone from any chat

WORKS WITHOUT SIGNAL
See what was last saved when you have no connection. Changes you make offline are sent automatically when you are back online.

YOUR APP, YOUR WAY
• Use the app in English, Afrikaans or isiZulu
• Choose a light or dark theme
• Choose which notifications you receive
• Sign in with Google, or with an email address and password
• Unlock the app with your fingerprint, face or screen lock

TutorBridge is for members of participating institutions. You can delete your account in Settings.
```
