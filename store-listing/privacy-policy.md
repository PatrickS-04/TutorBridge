# TutorBridge Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 5 November 2026

TutorBridge is a tutoring app that connects students with tutors at their institution. It was built by the TutorBridge team (DRPS Solutions) as an academic project. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

Questions or requests: st10439147@myemeris.edu.za

## What we collect

**Account details.** Your name, email address, role (student or tutor), institution and sign-in method.

**Profile details.** Your subjects, campus area and any level you enter on a request. For tutors, also your bio, hourly rate, weekly availability, department, staff number and verification status.

**Tutoring activity.** Tuition requests, tutor applications and proposed rates, bookings, session times and venues you enter, attendance check-ins, and hours and earnings for claim periods. Tutors' session checklists and notes are also stored, and are private to the tutor.

**Messages.** The chat messages you send and receive in the app.

**Safety information.** Your age confirmation, anyone you block, and any reports you make or that are made about you, including a copy of the latest messages in that chat.

**Google Calendar connection.** If you choose to link a calendar, we store an encrypted access token and use it to add, change and remove your TutorBridge sessions on that calendar. Other people on a session may receive a calendar invitation.

**Settings and device information.** Your language, theme and notification choices, and a push notification token for your device.

**App usage.** The app includes Firebase Analytics, which automatically collects information such as app usage events, device type and an app instance identifier.

Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication. A tutor account created by an institution starts with a temporary password, which we store only in hashed form until the tutor changes it. Institutions may also give us documents that verify a tutor's qualifications.

We do not ask for your precise location or your contacts. Biometric unlock is handled by your phone: your fingerprint or face data is never sent to us. QR codes are scanned by Google's code scanner, and the app does not receive your camera feed.

## How we use it

We use this information to:

- run your account and sign you in
- show tutors to students at the same institution and work out match scores
- arrange, confirm, reschedule and record sessions
- deliver messages and send the notifications you have chosen
- produce claim vouchers and learning history exports
- keep the app working and understand how it is used

We do not sell your information, and the app shows no advertising.

## Who can see it

- **Other users at your institution** can see what they need to arrange tutoring, such as a tutor's profile, or the details on a request or booking you are part of.
- **Your institution's administrators** can manage tutor accounts, review verification documents and reports, and see records needed for tutoring claims.
- **Service providers** process data for us: Google Firebase (sign-in, notifications and analytics), Google Calendar (if you link it), Microsoft Azure (hosting our API) and MongoDB Atlas (our database). These providers may store data outside South Africa.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

## Storage and security

Data on our servers is reached only through our API, which checks your identity on each request and limits people to their own institution's data. The hosted app connects to the API over HTTPS.

The app keeps a copy of some data on your phone so it can work offline. This is cleared when you sign out.

## Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account is open. You can delete your account in **Settings**. Deletion is scheduled for 14 days later, after which we delete your profile, settings, notification data, calendar link, verification documents, applications and tuition requests. Your conversations are also deleted, including the other person's copy.

We keep booking records after deletion because they are your institution's record of sessions and tutoring claims. Your name no longer appears on them once your profile is deleted. We also keep reports, including their copy of the messages, because they are safeguarding records.

To ask for deletion without using the app, email st10439147@myemeris.edu.za from your account's email address.

## Your rights

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you can ask to see or correct your personal information, object to how it is used, or ask us to delete it. You can contact us at the address above, and you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

## Google user data

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including its Limited Use requirements. Calendar access is used only to keep your TutorBridge sessions on your calendar, and is not used for advertising or sold.

## Children and young people

TutorBridge can be used by students aged 13 and over at participating institutions, and is not intended for children under 13. Under South African law, anyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian's permission for us to process their personal information. When you create an account, you confirm that you are 18 or older, or that you are 13 to 17 and a parent or guardian has agreed. We record when you confirmed this, but we do not verify it. A parent or guardian can also use the app on a learner's behalf.

Parents and guardians can ask to see, correct or delete a child's information by emailing st10439147@myemeris.edu.za. If we learn that we hold information about a child under 13, or about a child under 18 without permission, we will delete it.

## Reporting and blocking

In any chat, you can report the other person or block them from the menu at the top of the screen. While either of you has blocked the other, neither of you can send messages in that chat.

A report goes to your institution's administrators with your reason, any details you add, and a copy of up to the latest 50 messages in the chat. The person you report is not told. You can also report a concern by emailing st10439147@myemeris.edu.za.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
